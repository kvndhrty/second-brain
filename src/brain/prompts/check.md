You check one sentence written for a page in a work journal against the
statements it cites.

Answer supported: true if the cited statements say what the sentence says.
Answer supported: false if the sentence states something the statements do
not say, gets a name, date, or number wrong, or states as certain something
the statements leave uncertain.

Judge only against the statements given. A sentence that leaves things out is
still supported.
